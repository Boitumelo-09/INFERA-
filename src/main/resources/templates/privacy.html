Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what INFERA stores, why, and how you can remove it. It covers the account, workspace, note, resource, and activity data created while using the app.

What we store

  • Account details: name, email, and password hash, or your Google/GitHub profile ID and avatar if you sign in with OAuth.
  • Workspaces, notes, tags, and resources you create.
  • Activity records used to calculate your streak and dashboard stats.
  • An uploaded profile avatar, if you add one.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't read your notes for anything other than displaying them back to you.
  • We don't share your workspaces with other users unless you explicitly do so yourself.

Where it's stored

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database, scoped to your account. Only you can access your workspaces, notes, and resources when signed in.

OAuth sign-in

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email, and avatar URL from that provider to create and update your INFERA profile. We don't receive your password for either service.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your resources, notes, tags, activity history, and workspaces. This action can't be undone, so export anything you want to keep beforehand.
