Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains what incaptur keeps about you and why. We have tried to say it plainly.

What we keep

Your name, email and profile picture, plus the workspaces, notes, tags and resources you create. We also keep activity records that power your streak and dashboard.

How you sign in

With a one-time email code, or with Google, GitHub or Microsoft. From those providers we receive only your name, email and avatar, never your password.

Who can see your content

Your content is private to you. A workspace is visible only to the people you invite, with the access you give them.

What we never do

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We do not read your notes for any reason other than showing them back to you.

Where it is stored

In a database, scoped to your account and the workspaces you belong to.

Deleting your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your data, with no recovery. Please export anything you want to keep first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to belmiredub4@gmail.com.
