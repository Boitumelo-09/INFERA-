Why incaptur?

Questions people actually ask

Is incaptur free to use?

Yes. incaptur is free while it is in active development. If pricing is introduced later, anything you have already built stays yours to access.

How do I sign in?

With a one-time code sent to your email, or with Google, GitHub or Microsoft. There is no password to remember.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. Invite people to a workspace and give each one a role. Only the people you invite can see it.

Where is my data stored?

Your notes, resources and workspaces are stored in a database and scoped to your account and the workspaces you belong to.

What happens if I delete my account?

Everything in your account is permanently removed, with no recovery period. Export anything you want to keep first.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. incaptur is a responsive web app, so it works in your phone's browser today.
