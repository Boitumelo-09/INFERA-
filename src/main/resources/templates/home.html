Why choose INCAPTUR

A few honest reasons

01

It's built for structure, not just storage

Workspaces aren't a nice-to-have folder feature — they're the backbone of how INCAPTUR organizes everything else you do in it.

02

Tags work across your whole vault

A tag isn't trapped inside one workspace. Use the same tag in your coursework and your side project, and search still finds both.

03

Nothing here is a placeholder

If a feature is on the page, it works. No "coming soon" buttons, no fake integrations — what you see is what you can use today.

04

It's built by someone who uses it daily

INCAPTUR isn't a boardroom product. It's built by one developer who takes their own lecture notes and project notes in it — every decision comes from actually using the thing.
